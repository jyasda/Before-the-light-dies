# Before-the-light-dies
A tense first-person survival horror game where you manage your flashlight battery to survive. Running out triggers a countdown, don't let it reach 0. Scavenge for glowing batteries and reach the final door to escape. Its always watching... To play any version of the game (best is the most recent version), go to https://github.com/jyasda/Before-the-light-dies/releases , then download, extract, and click the "BeforeTheLightDies.exe" file. Enjoy!

Major updates for the next build: A ending, monster tweaks, and many bug fixes.
